# 🛒 Amazong: The Chaos Store

> **Live Website Demo:** [https://gorgeous-faun-aea149.netlify.app/]

---

Welcome, brave soul. You have stumbled upon **Amazong: The Chaos Store**, an intentionally frustrating, psychologically exhausting online shopping game designed to test your patience, sanity, and endurance to the absolute limit. 

Forget quick and easy checkout. Here, every click is a struggle, prices mutate based on your academic intelligence, and the website itself is your worst enemy.

---

## 🚀 Key Features of Psychological Torture

* **A Visual Neon Mess:** Built with blinding cyberpunk neon colors, retro scanlines, and wacky typography to give your eyes a hard time.
* **Familiar Yet Evil Categories:** Browse through electronics, beauty products, home decor, and fashion, all priced in Indian Rupees (₹) with hilarious, sarcastic product descriptions.
* **The 4-Click Cart Rule:** Trying to add an item to your cart? Good luck—the button will force you to click it 4 times while teasing you along the way.
* **Annoying Pop-Ups & Fake Errors:** Random popup windows, system dialog boxes, and sudden screen freezes will interrupt your flow and test your calm.
* **The "Intelligence Tax" & Interrogation Checkout:** 
  * You have to enter your academic grades (CGPA) before paying.
  * Answer 4 silly philosophical interrogation questions.
  * **The Catch:** Every word you type adds a strict **Word-Count Tax** (₹100 per word) to your final bill!
* **The Dodging Payment Button:** The final payment button literally runs away from your mouse cursor when you try to click it.
* **Certificate of Psychological Endurance:** Make it all the way to the end, and you'll get a downloadable, printable official certificate certifying that you survived maximum mental exhaustion.
* **Hall of Torture (Leaderboard):** Local storage-backed rankings that track how well you survived compared to other victims.

## 📌 How to View and Play This Project

Because this project is built using **pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript** (with zero external frameworks, build tools, or complicated setups), running it locally is effortless:

1. **Download the File:** Make sure your file is saved as **`index.html`**.
2. **Open in Any Browser:** Double-click the `index.html` file, or drag and drop it into any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.) to start playing instantly!
